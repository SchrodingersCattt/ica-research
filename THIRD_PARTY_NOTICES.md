# Third-party notices

This repository contains original workflow scripts and small scientific input/output examples. The following external projects are required by particular workflows and are not redistributed here:

- DeepMD-kit / DPA3: interatomic-potential inference and training.
- ASE: atomic simulation environment.
- Phonopy: finite-displacement phonons and thermal properties.
- SeeK-path: crystallographic Brillouin-zone paths.
- Pymatgen: elasticity analysis.
- CP2K: periodic electronic-structure calculations.
- LAMMPS: molecular dynamics and MSST implementation.
- ReacNetGenerator: reaction-network analysis of reactive trajectories.
- Multiwfn 3.8: IRI and sign(lambda2)rho analysis, referenced only.
- VMD/Tachyon: NCI/IRI rendering, referenced only.

Each project must be installed from its official distribution and remains under its own license. Version evidence and upstream links are recorded in `docs/software_versions.md`.
